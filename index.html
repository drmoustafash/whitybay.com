Questions from visitors

Do you speak English?

Yes. Dr. Moustafa and the team reply in English and Arabic, on WhatsApp and at the clinic.

Can I be treated during a one- or two-week holiday?

Often, yes. Whitening, cleaning, fillings and root canals usually take one or two visits. Veneers, crowns and implants need more visits, so send your dates first and Dr. Moustafa will tell you what fits.

How do I get a price before I travel?

Send a photo of your smile or a recent X-ray on WhatsApp. You get a treatment plan and a price for your case.

What if I get a toothache on holiday?

Message us on WhatsApp with where it hurts and your hotel. We give you the earliest appointment we have.

Where is the clinic?

In the city centre: El Nasr Street, El Dahar, 2nd floor above Anbar Pharmacy. Help with transfers is also possible.

How do I book?

On WhatsApp only. Tap any green button or fill in the short form below, and we reply with a time.
